Three decades of regenerating lives

Since 1987, MEBO International has transformed the treatment of burns and chronic wounds, establishing a new paradigm in regenerative medicine.

What Defines Us

Mission, vision and values

The principles that guide every decision at MEBO International and have allowed us to transform the lives of millions of patients for over three decades.

Mission

Provide regenerative therapeutic solutions that alleviate the suffering of patients with burns and chronic wounds, promoting the natural healing of skin without invasive surgery.

  • Global treatment accessibility
  • Certified pharmaceutical quality
  • Continuous professional training

Vision

To be the global benchmark in regenerative medicine, bringing Regenerative Medicine to every corner of the world where there is a patient who needs to regenerate their skin.

  • Expansion to 100+ countries
  • Leadership in regenerative research
  • Global standard of care

Values

Scientific innovation, respect for life, academic integrity and unwavering commitment to patient well-being in every decision.

  • Evidence-based science
  • Respect for human dignity
  • Integrity in all research
  • Commitment to the patient
Our Journey

MEBO International milestones

Nearly four decades of continuous innovation, global expansion, and commitment to regenerative science.

1987

Established

Professor Rongxiang Xu establishes the Guangming Burn and Ulcer Research Institute in Beijing — MEBO is founded.

Professor Rongxiang Xu, founder of MEBO
1988

First New Drug Certificate

MEBO Ointment receives the first new drug certificate for traditional Chinese medicine from China's Ministry of Health for burn treatment.

1991

Nationwide Adoption

Regenerative medicine is adopted in more than 4,000 hospitals in China, transforming the standard of burn treatment.

1992

First Medicine Approved & Patented

MEBO's first medicine is approved and patented, and Shantou MEBO is launched as the group's production base.

1995

U.S. Patent & First Overseas Markets

MEBO obtains the U.S. patent for its burn ointment formula. Classified as a nationwide treatment in China, the therapy launches in Syria, the UAE, South Korea and Thailand.

2000s

Global Presence

MEBO expands to more than 70 countries in Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Europe and Latin America, treating millions of patients.

2002

Scientific System Completed & FDA Trials Begin

The systematic science theory of regenerative medicine is fulfilled and announced, the first healthcare product is launched, and U.S. FDA clinical trials begin.

2007

Southeast Asia & Gulf Partnership

MEBO launches in Indonesia, the Philippines, New Zealand and beyond, and forms a strategic partnership with Gulf Pharmaceutical Industries (Julphar).

2011

Next Generation

Kevin Xu graduates from USC and assumes leadership as CEO of MEBO International, driving expansion in regenerative medicine and life sciences.

2014

UN Recognition

Kevin Xu receives the Empact 100 recognition at the United Nations, highlighting the impact of young entrepreneurs on the global economy.

2016

Academic Legacy

The Kevin Xu Chair in Gerontology is established at the USC Leonard Davis School. Kevin Xu is invited to the White House state dinner honoring Italian Prime Minister Matteo Renzi.

2019

FDA Clearance

MEBO Ointment receives FDA 510(k) clearance in the United States as a medical device for the treatment of burns. Kevin Xu receives the USC Young Alumni Merit Award.

Kevin Xu serves as Co-Chair of the APEC 2023 Host Committee in San Francisco, with MEBO International as an official enterprise partner. During the summit he attends Peruvian President Dina Boluarte's private dinner to prepare the handover of the APEC chairmanship to Peru. MEBO reaches presence in 73 countries.
