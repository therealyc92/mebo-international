Regenerative Medicine

Moist Exposed Burn Therapy represents a paradigm shift: instead of desiccating the wound, it creates an environment that mimics the natural physiological conditions of healthy skin.

Core Technology

From MEBT to Regenerative Medicine

In the 1980s, Prof. Rongxiang Xu introduced Moist Exposed Burn Therapy (MEBT) and Moist Exposed Burn Ointment (MEBO), transforming burn care in China. The breakthrough earned the State Technological Invention Award of China in 1988. Over the following decades, MEBT evolved into a complete regenerative platform — what is now recognised as Regenerative Medical Technology (RMT).

What began as a clinical innovation for burns has become a reproducible regenerative system. The same principles now restore tissue in chronic, traumatic and surgical wounds, and dermatological diseases — offering patients worldwide a genuine alternative to conventional care.

The Wounds We Treat

Acute vs. Chronic Wounds

Not all wounds heal alike. Understanding the difference explains why regenerative support matters.

Acute Wounds

Caused by surgery, trauma or burns, acute wounds follow an orderly repair sequence and typically close within 2–4 weeks. Proper management keeps this cascade on track and helps prevent progression to a chronic state.

MEBO edge

Immediate analgesia during dressing changes, and visibly less scarring.

Chronic Wounds

Chronic wounds stall in a prolonged inflammatory state and fail to close for more than 4 weeks — sometimes months or years. Most common: diabetic foot, venous leg and pressure ulcers.

MEBO edge

Unique liquefaction debridement that preserves viable tissue — and measurably faster closure in controlled trials.
