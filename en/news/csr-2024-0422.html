First Stop of APEC 2024 Activities: MEBO Group Visits ANIQUEM, Peru's Burned Children Rehabilitation Association

April 22, 2024|Posted by: MEBO International
CSRPERUANIQUEMCHILDREN

As the 2024 APEC series of activities prepared to open, MEBO Group traveled to Peru as an invited guest for an in-depth visit to ANIQUEM, the local burned children rehabilitation association.

Last November during the APEC Summit, Host Committee Chair Kevin Xu held a meaningful dialogue with Peruvian President Dina Boluarte on how MEBO Group could play a greater role in the upcoming APEC activities and contribute more to the international community — ultimately reaching a 2024 APEC cooperation agreement with the President and APEC organizing committee COMEXPERU.

COMEXPERU responded actively, preparing a series of related themed activities and sincerely inviting MEBO Group to visit local burn-related nonprofit organizations, aiming to share MEBO Group's rich resources and experience in burn treatment.

On April 19, MEBO Group Vice President Liu Gang led an international department delegation to Lima, the host city of APEC 2024, visiting ANIQUEM. The two sides held in-depth exchanges on deepening public-welfare cooperation in response to APEC themes and the CGI commitment.

ANIQUEM, Peru's only self-sustaining nonprofit NGO, has spent 25 years providing free comprehensive rehabilitation to child and adolescent burn survivors in poverty and extreme poverty — improving patients' quality of life while actively promoting prevention nationwide to reduce burn incidence. In the past year alone, ANIQUEM treated 2,859 patients, 50% of them children under five.

ANIQUEM's head, Ms. Cecilia, and her medical team warmly welcomed MEBO International, presenting the association's history and achievements. The delegation toured the physical rehabilitation area, psychological guidance area, children's activity center, the workshop for self-made elastic bandages, the hydrotherapy area, and the burn pressure mask display area — each demonstrating meticulous care and professional technique.

In the subsequent discussion, MEBO International expressed deep concern for local child patients and shared MEBO's founding story, the high compatibility of its products for burn patients, and the Group's outstanding record in public welfare and social responsibility. The two sides held lively discussions on donation implementation, physician technical training, and child care programs, agreeing to draft a proposal quickly and actively advance training and assistance activities — a meaningful visit that reflects MEBO's commitment as a mission-driven enterprise.
