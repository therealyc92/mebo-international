Regenerative Medical Technology Exchange in Peru Under the APEC Framework

November 21, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
MEDICALPERUAPECMEDICAL TRAINING

Under the ongoing influence of APEC's global cooperation framework, a MEBO International delegation conducted a five-day visit to Peru from November 17 to 21.

A series of medical exchange sessions was conducted for more than one hundred healthcare professionals across four major public hospitals: Hipólito Unanue National Hospital, Arzobispo Loayza National Hospital, Hospital Belén in Trujillo, and General Hospital Honorio Delgado Espinoza. The exchanges covered regenerative medical technology and its combined applications with other advanced treatment methods — such as microskin grafting and negative pressure wound therapy — as well as standardized wound dressing protocols using regenerative medical technology.

These sessions deepened healthcare professionals' understanding of wound management with regenerative medical technology, offering more efficient and groundbreaking solutions for acute and chronic wounds. Beyond the exchange sessions, the delegation also visited hospital wards to observe local medical personnel performing dressing changes with regenerative medical technology.

Continuing its engagement following the 2024 APEC events, the delegation also revisited ANIQUEM, the Peruvian charity for burned children. In recognition of MEBO International's outstanding contributions to raising local burn treatment standards, ANIQUEM presented the company with its 'Best Partner' award.

Moving forward, MEBO International will remain dedicated to its grand mission of 'Striving for Human Regenerative Life' — driven by innovative technology and guided by humanitarian principles, continuing to expand its global ecosystem and contributing enduring strength to sustainable development and human health worldwide.
