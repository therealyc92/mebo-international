Silicon Valley Meets APEC Again: 2025 Forum to Be Held in Gyeongju, South Korea

October 10, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
APECSILICON VALLEYAI GOVERNANCESOUTH KOREA

As the official host venue of APEC 2025, Gyeongju, South Korea is welcoming a series of major events. Among them, the 'Silicon Valley Meets APEC 2025 Forum' will be held on October 16 at The K Hotel Gyeongju, an official APEC partner hotel. Co-hosted by the Bay Area Council and MEBO International, this annual event is an APEC-recognized flagship side event, continuing the deep Silicon Valley–APEC cooperation tradition begun at the 2023 San Francisco meeting.

A defining feature of the forum is that it is not an isolated event but a year-long process of collective insight. In May 2025, the Sutter Health-hosted '2025 Bay Area–Silicon Valley Summit' gathered more than 300 leaders from government, business, academia, and nonprofits; in July, the Bay Area Council held preparatory discussions on AI policy, strategy, and regulation. The essence of these dialogues will be systematically distilled and released at the Gyeongju forum as a special report, 'AI Innovation and Regulation: A Silicon Valley Perspective'.

Under the theme 'AI Impact: Shaping the Future', the 2025 forum focuses on two frontier areas: global AI governance, and the integration of digital technology into healthcare. Kevin Xu — Bay Area Council executive committee member and former APEC Host Committee Chair — and Seong Woo Lee, Vice Chairman of the Korea Chamber of Commerce and Industry, will deliver remarks. Xu noted that the platform aims to synchronize the Bay Area–Silicon Valley economic ecosystem with APEC economies, creating scalable and replicable paths for sustainable development.

Highlights include a keynote by Microsoft's Director of Cybersecurity Policy John Hering on APEC's role in a global AI regulatory framework, the release of the special report by Sean Randolph of the Bay Area Council Economic Institute, and a panel on 'Healthcare × Digital Technology: Building a New Global Health Ecosystem' with representatives from MEBO Group, Deloitte, and Korea's Dong Wha Pharmaceutical.

Since the inaugural 'APEC Meets Silicon Valley' forum aboard the historic Klamath ferry in 2023 — later cited by the U.S. State Department as a key outcome of the San Francisco APEC — and the 2024 Lima summit under the 'APEC for the People' framework, the forum has become a vital bridge between Silicon Valley's innovation energy and APEC's multilateral policy coordination.
