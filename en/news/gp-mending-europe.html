ISBI and ABA Join 'Mending Europe: Empowering the Establishment of a Regeneration Network' Initiative

September 19, 2022|Posted by: MEBO International
GLOBAL PROGRAMSCGIEUROPEMEDICAL TRAINING

The International Society for Burn Injuries (ISBI) and the American Burn Association (ABA) have joined the Clinton Global Initiative (CGI) commitment 'Mending Europe: Empowering the Establishment of a Regeneration Network', launched by former U.S. President Bill Clinton and Kevin Xu, Chairman of the Board of MEBO Group.

ISBI and ABA will fulfill this initiative together with MEBO International and the International Society of Regenerative Medicine and Wound Repair (ISRMWR), delivering a series of training courses on burn prevention for both medical professionals and the public.

Previously, MEBO International had provided regenerative medicine training to medical professionals across Asia, Africa, and Latin America. 'Mending Europe' aims to establish a regeneration network in Europe by providing medical training, medical supplies, and funding — helping local medical facilities and professionals master regenerative medicine techniques and gain the ability to deliver medical treatment during emergencies and disasters.

Under this CGI commitment, MEBO International and ISRMWR have already provided online regenerative medicine training to more than 140 medical professionals from Bulgaria and Ukraine. Going forward, ISBI and ABA will cooperate with MEBO International to keep delivering more online and offline training across Europe.
