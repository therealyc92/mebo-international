MEBO Group Delegation Attends APEC 2025 Opening in Gyeongju, South Korea

October 29, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
APECSOUTH KOREACEO SUMMITDELEGATION

On October 29, 2025, the APEC Summit hosted by South Korea officially opened in Gyeongju — the country's first time hosting APEC in twenty years. The summit drew nearly 2,000 participants, including around one thousand CEO-level executives from the 21 APEC economies. As a Diamond Sponsor of this year's summit, MEBO Group board members Kevin Xu and Li Li led a 20-member delegation to the opening ceremony.

In his opening address, South Korean President Lee Jae-myung said: 'APEC is not only a platform for economic cooperation, but a stage on which we shape the future together. Facing the dual challenges of supply-chain uncertainty and digital transformation, members should join hands to promote sustainable and inclusive growth.'

Before the opening, the MEBO delegation attended the APEC 2025 CEO Summit welcome dinner at Gyeongju's Hwarang Village Harmony Square. Under the theme 'Bridge, Business, Beyond', the evening blended traditional Korean cultural elements with modern technology to open APEC Leaders' Week.

South Korean Prime Minister Kim Min-seok attended the dinner and exchanged views with Kevin Xu and Li Li, noting that no single country can meet today's challenges alone — only bridges between nations, businesses, academia, and civil society can bring closer connection, collaborative innovation, and shared prosperity. Xu echoed that the meaning of the APEC platform goes far beyond trade and economics: at its core are connection and partnership. MEBO Group Vice President Liu Gang also gave an exclusive interview to Korean broadcaster KBS on MEBO's three-year APEC strategy.

The summit is expected to generate economic benefits of around 7.4 trillion won, with Chairman Kevin Xu delivering a featured address on October 30.
