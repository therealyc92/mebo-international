Former APEC Host Committee Chair Kevin Xu Joins Panama Government Roundtable on Inter-American Connectivity and Innovation

September 23, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
APECPANAMAROUNDTABLEINTERCONNECTIVITY

On September 22, 2025 (New York time), during UN General Assembly week, the Government of Panama successfully held a high-level roundtable forum in New York themed 'Promoting Connectivity and Innovation for the Americas'. Panamanian President José Raúl Mulino attended and delivered a keynote address. Kevin Xu, Chairman of MEBO Group and former Chair of the APEC Host Committee, attended as a specially invited guest.

The forum focused on connectivity and innovation across the Americas, bringing together government officials, business leaders, and representatives of international organizations to explore cooperation in the digital economy, sustainable development, and international public welfare.

Xu pointed out that Panama hosts one of the world's few major humanitarian relief hubs, with significant locational advantages. He expressed hope that cooperation and innovation could reactivate the hub's functions, making it a vital center for disaster relief, medical resource coordination, and response serving Latin America and the world — greatly improving global emergency rescue efficiency and medical resource allocation.

Xu's engagement with the Americas dates back to 2019: his first official visit to Panama that January, and the June 2019 Clinton Foundation CGI gathering where the 'Regenerative Wave — MEBT Training in Latin America' commitment was announced. Building on the success of APEC Peru 2024, MEBO has continued to carry forward APEC's open and shared spirit through the Latin America Regenerative Medical Technology Symposium series in Peru and beyond.

Xu's renewed invitation to Panama's high-level forum reflects the continued recognition earned by MEBO in international collaboration and regional development — and signals new opportunities for connectivity and innovation cooperation across Central and Latin America.
