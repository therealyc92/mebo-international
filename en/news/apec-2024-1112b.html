Inside the APEC 2024 Official Gift Bag: Unique Design, Rich Culture

November 12, 2024|Posted by: MEBO International
APECPERUCULTUREMEBO PRODUCTS

On November 9, the APEC 2024 Leaders' Week opened in Peru, and the carefully prepared official gift bag quickly drew attention. Its design is inspired by Peru's famous natural wonder, Rainbow Mountain — a concept that showcases the country's natural beauty while blending the diverse cultures and cooperative spirit of APEC member economies.

Inside the bag: a baseball cap bearing the APEC CEO Summit logo; an elegant leather passport holder; a brooch of the endangered purple-striped alstroemeria that grows on the hills around Lima; a keychain modeled on Peru's treasured vicuña; a handicraft made of 100% alpaca wool showcasing local artistry; a booklet of local cuisine; and — a symbol of friendship — a panda pin, signifying the friendly relations and close cooperation among APEC members.

Notably, the Peru edition of MEBO Moist Exposed Burn Ointment was also included in the gift bag. As the only pharmaceutical product featured, it demonstrates MEBO's achievements in botanical medicine and MEBO International's unique contribution to health cooperation and exchange among APEC member economies.

More than a fine souvenir, the APEC 2024 gift bag is a precious gift carrying diverse cultures and deep friendship — a witness to the close cooperation and shared development of APEC members, and a distinctive APEC memory for every delegate.
