MEBO Reopens the APEC 2025 Moment

June 30, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
APECCEO SUMMITROADSHOWPARTNERSHIP

On June 30, 2025, the global roadshow of the APEC 2025 CEO Summit — 'Bridging Asia-Pacific Development Prospects', the core prelude to the year's APEC series — officially launched, gathering business organization leaders and policy experts from across the Asia-Pacific.

Attendees included Seong Woo Lee, Vice Chairman of the Korea Chamber of Commerce and Industry and Secretary-General of the APEC CEO Summit Secretariat; Monica Hardy Whaley, President of the U.S. National Center for APEC; and Zhang Shaogang, ABAC member for China. Andrew Dy, General Manager of the Asia Business Forum, moderated the opening.

Kevin Xu, former APEC Host Committee Chair and Chairman of MEBO Group, joined a high-level roundtable alongside senior leaders from finance, healthcare, new energy, and the broader health industry — including Catherine Simmons of Citibank and Monica He of Organon. Drawing on his deep involvement in APEC 2023 and 2024, Xu shared insights on how innovative cooperation models and sustainable business practices can genuinely 'bridge' the development needs of Asia-Pacific economies. He emphasized that the APEC platform's meaning goes far beyond trade and economics — its openness builds bridges for dialogue and co-created solutions, which is also the starting point of corporate social responsibility. As a mission-driven company, MEBO will continue building standardized medical networks to benefit all, serve refugees, and withstand disasters.

The roadshow also featured the announcement of the summit's global sponsors, with MEBO Group Vice President Liu Gang signing the strategic cooperation agreement on behalf of the Group — sounding the opening note for the APEC 2025 CEO Summit in South Korea and injecting new momentum into practical cooperation under the APEC framework.
