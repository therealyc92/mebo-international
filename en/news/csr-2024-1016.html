MEBO APEC 2024 in Action: Partnering with ANIQUEM to Build a Bridge of Care for Children

October 16, 2024|Posted by: MEBO International
CSRPERUCHILDRENBURN CARE

As the APEC 2024 Summit approaches, MEBO Group — a key strategic partner of two consecutive APEC summits (2023 and 2024) — is demonstrating through concrete action Chairman Kevin Xu's firm commitment to the APEC ideals of cooperation, legacy, and sharing.

On October 15, 2024, MEBO Group joined Peruvian nonprofit ANIQUEM to launch the Regenerative Life Action caring project for burned children. The initiative expresses deep humanitarian concern for burned children in Latin America and powerfully advances pediatric burn prevention and treatment systems across the APEC region. Since Xu successfully placed burn care on the APEC agenda in 2023, he has continued driving concrete action through regional cooperation and resource sharing, putting child burn injuries in the APEC region front and center.

Xu returned to Peru after his October 3 APEC venue tour to attend the launch ceremony and deliver remarks. He noted that burn treatment may be only one corner of medicine, yet it carries great love and responsibility — affecting not only individual quality of life but the harmony and stability of families and society. He expressed hope that the APEC stage can bring all parties together to advance the innovation and popularization of burn treatment technology, creating a safer, healthier environment for children.

Dr. Raúl Rodríguez Vilca, President and founder of ANIQUEM, shared the organization's remarkable achievements in burn prevention and treatment, emphasizing that the ANIQUEM-MEBO partnership brings hope for surgical correction of burn sequelae and comprehensive rehabilitation for children and adolescents living in poverty and extreme poverty. ANIQUEM is a nonprofit providing free comprehensive rehabilitation to child and adolescent burn survivors in poverty.

APEC CEO Summit Executive Director Luciano Paredes praised the MEBO-ANIQUEM alliance as a model of enterprise-civil society cooperation toward shared goals — a vivid practice of APEC's commitment to 'prioritizing people's well-being and development' and an important step in regional health cooperation. At the ceremony, MEBO Group Vice President Liu Gang and Dr. Rodríguez Vilca signed the cooperation agreement: through physical therapy, occupational therapy, psychological support, and custom pressure garments, the two sides will provide all-round rehabilitation and build a culture of burn prevention for Peruvian families.

At the event, the 'Little MEBO' panda toys prepared by MEBO Group were adored by the children, becoming messengers of friendship. The children painted freely on canvas, depicting their hopes for the future in brilliant colors. Most touchingly, Valentina created a colorful heart-shaped painting and presented it to Chairman Xu in gratitude — adding a warm highlight to this meaningful occasion. The Peru launch of the Regenerative Life Action children's burn project marks the grand opening of MEBO's APEC 2024 series of actions.
