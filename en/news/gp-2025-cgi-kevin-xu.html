MEBO Group Chairman Kevin Xu Announces New Global Health Initiative at CGI Health Working Group

September 24, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
GLOBAL PROGRAMSCGISILVER HEALINGELDERLY CARE

On September 24, 2025, Kevin Xu, Chairman of the Board of MEBO Group, was invited to the Clinton Global Initiative (CGI) Health Working Group meeting, where he officially launched a new global program — 'Silver Healing: Equitable Community Wound Care'.

The initiative aims to extend regenerative medical technology from professional clinical settings to community-level healthcare, providing accessible and sustainable wound care solutions for elderly patients worldwide. The program is planned for implementation across 28 countries and regions within the Asia-Pacific, ASEAN, and the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) from 2025 to 2028, seeking to improve the quality of care for tens of thousands of elderly patients with chronic wounds through community wound therapist training, health education campaigns, and medical donations.

This initiative marks another milestone in the decade-long partnership between MEBO Group and CGI. Since 2015, the two sides have jointly advanced regenerative medical technology worldwide through medical training, humanitarian aid, and youth leadership development — from large-scale doctor training in China to regenerative medical technology initiatives across Latin America and Europe, as well as youth innovation projects during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Over the years, former U.S. President and CGI Founder Bill Clinton has repeatedly praised Kevin Xu and MEBO Group for their outstanding global contributions. This decade-long collaboration has not only accelerated the internationalization of regenerative medical technology but also embodied the integration of scientific innovation, talent cultivation, and humanitarian action — fulfilling the mission of 'Striving for Human Regenerative Life'.
