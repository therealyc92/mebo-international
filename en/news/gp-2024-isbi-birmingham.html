22nd ISBI Congress Successfully Held in Birmingham

August 22, 2024|Posted by: MEBO International
GLOBAL PROGRAMSISBIBURN CAREUNITED KINGDOM

The 22nd Congress of the International Society for Burn Injuries (ISBI) took place in Birmingham, UK, from August 18 to 22, 2024, under the theme 'Exchanging Knowledge to Improve Burn Care Around the World — Facing the Future'. Co-hosted by the British Burn Association, the event brought together more than 1,000 burn care specialists from 47 countries to share insights and collaborate on improving global burn care practices.

ISBI President-elect David Harrington opened the congress, followed by current ISBI President Folke Sjöberg, who outlined upcoming initiatives including the ISBI Burn Center Audit Program.

MEBO International was prominent at the congress, showcasing regenerative medical technology. The company's innovative exhibition highlighted its contributions to APEC, the Kevin Xu Initiative, and global training programs, attracting significant attention from medical professionals.

On the first day, MEBO International hosted a seminar titled 'Regenerative Medicine: The Future of Wound Management', featuring a range of speakers with notable presentations on cutting-edge treatments for facial burns and large-scale burn injuries. The second day featured a keynote by Gerald Abesamis highlighting the clinical benefits of Moist Exposed Burn Therapy (MEBT) for facial burns.

The ISBI Congress remains one of the premier events in global burn care, and MEBO International's contributions at the 2024 congress further solidified the role of regenerative medicine on the world stage. The next congress, scheduled for 2026 in Santiago, Chile, promises to continue this momentum.
