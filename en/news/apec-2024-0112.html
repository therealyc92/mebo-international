U.S. State Department Releases 2023 APEC Outcomes Report, Bay Area Council Activities Listed as Key Results

January 12, 2024|Posted by: MEBO International
APECU.S. STATE DEPARTMENTBAY AREA COUNCILOUTCOMES

According to the 2023 APEC outcomes report released by the U.S. State Department, important results were achieved around the host year's priorities of sustainability and inclusivity, digital innovation, and resilient and inclusive growth. Key deliverables include the Manoa Agenda for a sustainable and inclusive economy, the Pacific Agenda on digitalization, and the Bay Area Council agenda on resilient and inclusive growth.

During APEC Week, the Bay Area Council hosted a five-day APEC California and Silicon Valley conference designed to deepen APEC economies' engagement in the Bay Area and support the long-term development of APEC's key priorities. From November 13–17, aboard the historic Klamath ferry at San Francisco's Pier 9, senior representatives from APEC economies and San Francisco/Silicon Valley business leaders exchanged views on trade, investment, technology, innovation, and climate resilience — providing an occasion for business and government leaders from many countries to discuss the region's challenges and opportunities.

Kevin Xu — APEC Host Committee Chair, Bay Area Council director, Co-Chair of its International Committee, and head of its APEC working group — played a full coordinating role throughout, integrating forces from all sectors, gathering broad input, and pooling resources across business, academia, and government, enriching the content of APEC meetings and amplifying the influence of APEC 2023 across multiple platforms and formats.

During APEC Week, civil society organizations and the San Francisco APEC Host Committee hosted nearly 30 public and stakeholder engagement initiatives, attended by more than 3,000 senior government officials and global business leaders — including the APEC Multi-Stakeholder Forum, the APEC University Leaders Forum, and APEC Study Centers conferences. Some 1,200 multilingual volunteers — American and international students, retirees, and citizens from across the United States — also played a vital role in the week's success.
