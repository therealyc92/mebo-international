4th APEC Silicon Valley Summit Concludes in Hong Kong, Empowering Asia-Pacific Collaboration

June 2, 2026|Posted by: MEBO International
APECSILICON VALLEY SUMMITHONG KONG, CHINADIGITAL ECONOMY

On June 1, 2026, the 'Silicon Valley Meets APEC: Bay to Bay' cooperation forum — the 4th APEC Silicon Valley Summit, co-hosted by the Bay Area Council and MEBO International — was successfully held at the University of Hong Kong's iCube Innovation Center. The forum gathered leading experts from business, technology innovation, higher education, policy research, and innovation ecosystems across Hong Kong and the Asia-Pacific, alongside officials from ABAC and PECC member economies, to discuss AI industrialization, the digital economy, and cross-regional collaborative innovation.

Kevin Xu — Chair of the APEC 2023 Host Committee, Bay Area Council director, and Chairman of MEBO Group — delivered opening remarks, interpreting APEC's core value as a highly cohesive and effective multilateral platform. Since the San Francisco APEC Summit in 2023 established a standing dialogue between Silicon Valley and APEC, the two sides have built a stable, efficient exchange system. Landing in Hong Kong, this year's event built a two-way bridge between the San Francisco Bay Area and China's Greater Bay Area, connecting policy research institutions, top young talent, and private-sector players.

Xu further noted that with China officially hosting APEC in 2026, the region faces new development opportunities. As an international free port and Asia-Pacific innovation hub, Hong Kong's hosting of the fourth 'Silicon Valley Meets APEC' dialogue opens a high-quality prelude to the full APEC 2026 series. Dr. Sean Randolph, Senior Director of the Bay Area Council Economic Institute, delivered a keynote on how AI is reshaping the global economic landscape, and panelists discussed building AI-driven economies and strengthening Hong Kong's position as an international innovation and technology hub.

The same day, a healthcare innovation closed-door dinner co-hosted by the Clinton Global Initiative (CGI) and MEBO International was held at the Hong Kong Club, with Chelsea Clinton in attendance. In a fireside chat with veteran journalist Brian Calle, she explored Hong Kong's advantages as a top-tier Asian healthcare innovation hub, responses to global climate and public-health crises, the application and ethics of AI in medicine, and the role of youth leadership in global health.

During the series, MEBO International and the NCAPEC Foundation signed a high-level strategic cooperation memorandum covering international public communication, cross-regional dialogue, cultural exchange, and sustainable development. The events were supported by HKU's Jockey Club Global Sustainability Institute, CGI, the Xu Peng Think Tank at the University of Chicago, Equitech Futures, and the Sacramento-China Office, among others.
