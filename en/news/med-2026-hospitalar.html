MEBO International at Hospitalar 2026 Brazil: Expanding Regenerative Medical Technology into Latin America

May 19, 2026|Posted by: MEBO International
MEDLATIN AMERICABRAZILWOUND CARE

The 2026 Brazil International Medical Equipment Exhibition (Hospitalar) was held on May 19 in São Paulo, Brazil. As one of the most influential medical industry exhibitions in Latin America, Hospitalar brought together global institutions and companies across medical equipment, medical technology, and hospital management, providing a platform for international cooperation and market exchange.

The MEBO International team showcased Regenerative Medical Technology (RMT) and related products for the first time in the Brazilian market, focusing on wound care applications, clinical cases, and product usage solutions — attracting strong interest from attending professionals.

During the exhibition, pharmaceutical distributors from Brazil, Venezuela, Paraguay, Peru, Chile, the Dominican Republic, Bolivia, and Argentina expressed strong interest in regenerative life sciences and held discussions on product registration, market cooperation, and technology promotion. Local doctors, nurses, and wound care professionals engaged in extensive exchanges on RMT concepts and natural-ingredient wound care solutions, sparked by real case presentations and treatment outcomes.

MEBO International also presented its Glymate premium health product series, focused on gastrointestinal health and blood glucose management, and reached a preliminary cooperation intention with a local pharmaceutical company. The two parties plan to initiate clinical trial projects on wound care and evaluate product application effects through real cases. MEBO also completed a preliminary feasibility assessment for Brazilian market access with local regulatory consultants.

With a population of over 200 million, Brazil has significant needs in wound care and chronic wound management. Going forward, MEBO International will continue to promote RMT cooperation and exchange across Latin America, providing safe, effective, and accessible wound care solutions for local patients.
