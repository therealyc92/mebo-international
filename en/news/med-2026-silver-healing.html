MEBO International Continues to Promote Regional Medical Exchanges, Focusing on Silver Healing

April 18, 2026|Posted by: MEBO International
MEDICALSILVER HEALINGPERUELDERLY CARE

In 2025, MEBO International joined hands with its Peruvian partner Discontinental to successfully launch the Latin America Regenerative Medical Technology Seminar program, which received widespread acclaim. In 2026, MEBO International is continuing the program with six seminars planned for the year — two of them closely aligned with the Silver Healing initiative jointly launched by MEBO International and the Clinton Foundation.

The second seminar was held on April 18, 2026, focusing on the application of regenerative medical technology in the treatment of elderly wounds. It aligns closely with MEBO International's 'Silver Healing: Building an Equitable Community Care System for Chronic Wounds' initiative, integrating specialized technical training with grassroots community medical programs to support the accessibility of medical resources and health equity.

The seminar specially invited Kenai Benítez Salgado, founder of Mexico's MediCalli primary care program, and Yu Ran, Head Nurse of the Wound Care Center at Nanyang Nanshi Hospital, to deliver special presentations — deeply integrating Silver Healing medical training with local community healthcare entrepreneurship projects and effectively enhancing the accessibility of primary care services.

Aimed primarily at community healthcare workers from Peru's Padomi home care project, the seminar drew more than 80 in-person participants, plus online attendees from Argentina, Costa Rica, and other Latin American countries, to an enthusiastic response.

The first two seminars have provided Latin American healthcare workers with new approaches to wound treatment and expanded the practical application of regenerative medical technology in community care. In the second half of the year, MEBO International will organize four more seminars, steadily advancing the rooting and growth of regenerative medicine in Latin America — bringing lasting, positive change to public health in the region and around the world.
