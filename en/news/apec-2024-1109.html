Silicon Valley Wisdom Meets Asia-Pacific Strength: APEC 2024 Silicon Valley Summit Comes to Lima

November 9, 2024|Posted by: MEBO International
APECSILICON VALLEY SUMMITPERUINVITATION

As the APEC Summit convenes in Peru from November 13–15, 2024, Kevin Xu — Chairman of MEBO Group and Chair of the APEC 2023 Host Committee — extended warm greetings to the world and a heartfelt invitation to the APEC 2024 Silicon Valley Summit, co-hosted by the Bay Area Council and MEBO International.

In the welcome video, Xu emphasized: 'APEC is not only an outstanding platform for trade, economics, and cooperation — it is also a gathering where we can explore entertainment, culture, and education. It is a rare opportunity to meet friends and future partners here, and to weave bridges of cooperation together.'

During APEC 2023, the U.S. government named the APEC Silicon Valley Forum among the year's highlighted achievements. As an important official activity under APEC 2024's 'APEC for the People' framework, the APEC 2024 Silicon Valley Summit will be held on November 9 in Lima, jointly organized by the Bay Area Council and MEBO International.

As a key strategic partner of two consecutive APEC summits, MEBO International has been committed to advancing technology cooperation and development across the Asia-Pacific. At the summit, participants will explore how technological innovation can address global challenges and drive sustainable development.
