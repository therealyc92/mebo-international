MEBO Chairman Kevin Xu Attends the 2025 BRICS Business Forum

July 6, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
GLOBAL PROGRAMSBRICSBRAZILSUMMIT

On July 5, 2025, Kevin Xu, Chairman of MEBO Group, attended the BRICS Business Forum in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, by invitation. The forum, a core side event of the BRICS Leaders' Meeting, focused on four themes — trade security, energy transition, digital technology empowerment, and cross-border financing strategy — building a resource-integration platform for emerging economies.

Recognized for its long-term contributions to regional rule-building and resource sharing, MEBO Group joined this year's forum as a strategic partner. Xu attended as former APEC Host Committee Chair, at the special invitation of COP30 President Ambassador André Corrêa do Lago.

During the forum, Xu met with Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim; the two agreed to hold further dialogue during the APEC CEO Summit in South Korea in October 2025 to advance regional economic cooperation. Xu also exchanged views with senior political figures from Brazil, Kazakhstan, the United Arab Emirates, and other countries and regions.

This was Xu's second BRICS Business Forum: at the 2017 Xiamen forum, he heard President Xi Jinping's keynote 'Deepening the BRICS Partnership and Opening a Brighter Future' and joined economic and trade discussions. This renewed deep participation extends Xu's practice of cross-regional cooperation under the APEC framework, echoes his call to 'strengthen the sense of belonging to an Asia-Pacific community with a shared future', and demonstrates MEBO Group's growing and sustained influence in the development of emerging economies.

Brazilian President Lula, CNI President Ricardo Alban, BRICS Business Council Chairman Francisco Neto, BRICS Women's Business Alliance Chair Monica Monteiro, Ambassador André Corrêa do Lago, BNDES President Aloizio Mercadante, and Rio Mayor Eduardo Paes were among the political, business, and academic leaders in attendance. Founded in 2010 in Rio de Janeiro, the BRICS Business Forum promotes dialogue among member business communities, deepens economic and trade cooperation, and conveys business priorities to governments.
