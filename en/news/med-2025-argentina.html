MEBO International Participates in Argentina Wound Conference, Promoting Regenerative Medical Technology Across Latin America

November 15, 2025|Posted by: MEBO International
MEDARGENTINAWOUND CARECONFERENCE

From November 11 to 15, 2025, MEBO International traveled to Buenos Aires, Argentina for five days of academic exchange and market research. The team visited the Hospital de Clínicas 'José de San Martín', affiliated with the University of Buenos Aires, and conducted research at several chain pharmacies, gaining in-depth insight into the local healthcare system and market characteristics.

The team also participated in the Wound, Ostomy, and Incontinence Conference, comprehensively promoting the dissemination of regenerative medical technology in the region. At the opening ceremony, Migra López, President of the Latin American Multidisciplinary Coalition for Wounds, Ostomy, and Incontinence (COMLHEI), emphasized that the event's significance lies in cross-border exchange and multidisciplinary integration. Ariel Miranda, Secretary-General and President-Elect of the International Society for Burn Injuries (ISBI), called for a more open environment for global medical exchange.

During the conference, Xu Weiwei, Director of the Medical Department at MEBO International, delivered two lectures on the wound microenvironment and regenerative medical technology. She emphasized the critical role of microenvironmental factors in wound healing and presented evidence-based data demonstrating the efficacy of regenerative medical technology in both acute and chronic wounds.

At the exhibition, MEBO International showcased its key products and technologies. Ariel Miranda's visit to the booth and in-depth discussion with the team highlighted the strong interest of Latin American medicine in innovative regenerative solutions.

This visit to Argentina deepened MEBO International's insight into the local market and advanced the dissemination of regenerative medical technology in Latin America. Moving forward, MEBO International will continue collaborating with global partners to deliver higher-quality regenerative medical solutions that benefit more patients.
