Overview

Topical Ointment for Burn & Wounds

MEBO Wound Ointment is a topical medicine for treating burns and wounds. It combines multiple naturally-derived active ingredients with a patented dosage form.

The ointment provides a physiological moist environment, liquefies and removes necrotic tissues, reduces pain, prevents and controls infection, prevents and minimizes scar formation, and promotes in situ skin regeneration.

What Is Inside

The Base: A Frame That Carries the Actives

Frame structure — beeswax

Beeswax builds a honeycomb-like frame that holds the oil phase in place, so the ointment stays on the wound and releases actives steadily.

Droplets — sesame oil, avg. 19 μm

Micro-droplets of sesame oil carry all active constituents and help maintain the physiological moist environment the wound needs.

How to Apply MEBO

Three application methods, chosen according to the wound type and condition. Clean the wound gently — never use sterile solutions or antiseptics.

1st-degree burns Every 4–6 hours
Step 1: Clean wound

Gently remove liquefied necrotic tissue and exudate with gauze or forceps.

Step 2: Apply MEBO

Cover the wound with a ~1 mm layer of MEBO.

2nd-degree burns Every 8–12 hours
Step 1: Prepare impregnated gauze

Mix sterile gauze with MEBO until fully impregnated.

Step 2: Remove old dressing

Gently lift the used gauze off the wound.

Step 3: Clean wound

Remove liquefied necrotic tissue and exudate.

Step 4: Apply & cover

Apply 1–2 mm of MEBO, then cover with 1–2 layers of impregnated gauze.

3rd-degree burns & chronic wounds Every 12–24 hours
Step 1: Prepare impregnated gauze

Mix sterile gauze with MEBO until fully impregnated.

Step 2: Remove old dressing

Gently lift the used gauze off the wound.

Step 3: Clean wound

Remove liquefied necrotic tissue and exudate.

Step 4: Apply MEBO

Cover the wound with 2–3 mm of MEBO.

Step 5: Cover layers

Apply 2–4 layers of MEBO-impregnated gauze.

Step 6: Pad & secure

Add a 1–3 cm cotton pad and bandage with light pressure.
